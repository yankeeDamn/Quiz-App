Questions

Before you start

Do students need to install an app?

No. Tests run in the phone browser. Students open your institute’s link, log in with their mobile number and start.

Can I use my own logo and colours?

Yes. Each institute has its own portal with its name, logo and colour on every page, in the exam window and on certificates. Try it in the portal builder above.

How do students ask doubts?

Each question in the review has a WhatsApp button. It opens a chat with your counsellor or teacher with the question, the student’s answer and the correct answer already filled in. You can also add a batch discussion group link.

Can I upload my own questions?

Yes. Teachers add questions one by one or upload a CSV with options, the right answer, an explanation and a topic tag. This page ships with a ready library so you can see how tests feel.

Is my institute’s data separate from other institutes?

Yes. Every institute is its own tenant with its own students, batches and results. Another institute cannot see yours.

How do I pay for a plan?

Choose a plan and Exampro opens WhatsApp with your choice written out. We confirm the plan there and share payment details. Yearly billing costs ten months’ price.

Does this demo save my data?

Attempts, bookmarks, your streak and the sign-in stay in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and clearing site data removes them. Mobile-OTP login arrives with your institute portal.

Which exam patterns are supported?

You set marks per question, negative marking, sections and time. The library includes SSC, banking, railway, UPSC and state PSC, and campus placement patterns as starting points.
